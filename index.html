A brief introduction

Hi! Name's
Luca.

I'm an Italian 2D Animator and Storyboard Artist based in Milan.

I graduated from NABA in 2025 with a degree in Cinema and Animation, where I learned how to draw characters and bring them to life through different animation techniques: paperless, traditional hand-drawn, cutout, and even stop-motion.

I specialize in character animation, but I also work on storyboards and animatics, always putting storytelling and expressive action front and center.
